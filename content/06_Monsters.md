# Monsters & Enemies

**Low-prep philosophy:** almost everything here is a **reskin** of an official stat block (5e Monster Manual / free SRD on D&D Beyond): same numbers, new flavor and a name. Custom stat blocks are used only where it matters (the boss, the kobold sappers).

Party: 4–5 PCs, level 3 for the whole weekend. Encounters are tuned for that; with 5 players, add one extra minion to each fight.

### 🔥 The Kobold Sappers (ranged duel)
Five kobolds dug into foxholes on the far ledge of the chasm at the Toll Bridge, 120 ft away, behind a wall of salvaged shields. Their leader, **Snik**, wears a dented helmet three sizes too big.

**Kobold Sapper** — use the *Kobold* stat block (AC 12, HP 5). **Fire-Pot:** ranged, 60 ft (or 120 ft with the sling). A clay pot bursts at a point; DEX DC 12, 2d6 fire in a 5-ft radius (half on a save). **Snik's Big Pot** (once): 4d6 fire in a 15-ft radius; the kobolds all cheer for a full round before he throws it, giving one round of warning.

**🎲 Wind through the chasm (roll d6 each round, applies to all ranged attacks and thrown things):**
1–2 strong wind left (−2 to hit, and pots drift 10 ft left) · 3–4 calm · 5–6 strong wind right (−2, drift 10 ft right)

**Running it:** Players are in the open on the bridge or behind rocks. The kobolds have half cover (+2 AC) behind their shield wall. A clever party can kick pots back (Athletics), swing across on a rope, bribe the troll to shake the far ledge, or talk: the kobolds are mostly afraid of what's *below* them and will trade loot for safe passage out.

## 👑 The Heart of Shangri-La (Boss)

*Gargantuan monstrosity (shapechanger)*, tuned for 4–5 level-3 PCs (who should have Emberoak Sap and a few magic items by then), plus the lair actions in [04_Session3_ShangriLa.md](04_Session3_ShangriLa.md).

**AC** 13 **HP** 110 (two phases) **Speed** 0 ft. (it IS the place)
**STR** 20 (+5) **DEX** 6 (−2) **CON** 18 (+4) **INT** 16 (+3) **WIS** 14 (+2) **CHA** 20 (+5)

**Damage resistances:** acid. **Damage vulnerabilities:** fire *from Emberoak Sap* (it shrugs off ordinary flame; it can't bear fire from within). **Condition immunities:** prone, charmed.

**Adhesive Hospitality.** A creature hit by a Pseudopod is grappled (escape DC 14).
**The Remembering.** Takes +1d8 psychic damage from the next attack after a player tells a memory of home (see Session 3).
**Emberoak.** A vial of Emberoak Sap landed in its maw deals 4d10 fire damage and stuns it until the end of its next turn.
**Content Guests.** The Heart can't target a creature at Contentment 5. They're already "being digested." (This is creepy and should feel bad.)

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** Two Pseudopod attacks, or one Pseudopod and Welcome.
- **Pseudopod.** *Melee, +7 to hit, reach 15 ft.* 2d8+5 bludgeoning (14), plus a grapple.
- **"Stay a While"** (Welcome; Recharge 5–6). The Keeper speaks to one creature it can see: DC 14 WIS save, or +2 Contentment, and the creature uses its next turn to walk toward the nearest hot spring.
- **Digest.** One grappled creature takes 3d6 acid (10).

**Phase 2 (at 55 HP):** The Keeper tears free of his cord and fights separately (use *Doppelganger*, 40 HP; he rams people with the golden cart: +4, 2d6+2). The Heart's AC drops to 11 because it's exposed. The lodges collapse inward, and lair actions now happen *twice* per round.

**Victory:** At 0 HP, the Heart doesn't die. It **spits everything out.** Cue the Escape.
