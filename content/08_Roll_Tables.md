# Roll Tables

**🧑 = Player rolls** (hand them the die and read the result dramatically). **🎩 = DM rolls.**
Feel free to *rig* a roll if a better story is obvious. The dice are there to inspire you, not to rule you.

> **Tool tip:** On the DM screen app, every table here has a Roll button. On paper, print the player tables on cards and let players read their own results aloud.

---

## 🧑 Fumble Table (d10) — *optional, friendly* — roll on a natural 1 in combat

| d10 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Your weapon slips and lands 10 ft away. |
| 2 | You shout a battle cry so strange that everyone (enemies included) pauses. No effect. |
| 3 | You trip. Prone. |
| 4 | You hit an adjacent ally for 1 damage. Apologize in character. |
| 5–8 | Just a miss, but describe it as stylishly as possible. |
| 9 | You miss so badly the enemy laughs: it has disadvantage on its next attack. |
| 10 | Your miss somehow sets up an ally perfectly: they get +2 on their next attack. |
