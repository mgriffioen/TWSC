# Session 2 — The Undercroft (Saturday Afternoon)

**Length:** 3–4 hours  **Level:** 3  **Goal:** A classic dungeon crawl with strange monsters, puzzles, choices and rising dread. Every room is a little *warmer* and a little more like summer. It ends at the Golden Gate.

## Before you start: "The Morning After" 🎲

1. Each player rolls on **"What Did You Do Last Night?"** ([08_Roll_Tables.md](08_Roll_Tables.md)). They wake at the bottom of the sinkhole, bruised, and each discovers a consequence of the festival night.
2. Anyone who ate at **Fisherman's Luck** makes a DC 12 CON save. On a failure they are poisoned for the first hour (real time).
3. Note who carries the **Emberoak Sap** vial from Ham Point (if they got it).

## Map Structure (node-based, not grid)

Keep the dungeon as a **flowchart** of rooms. Draw it on a big sheet or whiteboard and reveal rooms as the party reaches them.

```
                  [1] The Landing (sinkhole bottom)
                        │          (the Ham Point sea-stair also arrives here)
        ┌───────────────┼────────────────┐
  [2] Bullywug     [3] Fishfolk      [4] The Three Oaks
      Hollow           Camp              (steam hall)
        │               │                │
        └───────┬───────┘                │
          [5] The Toll Bridge ───────────┘
                │   (kobold sappers across the chasm)
          [6] The Mother's Grave
                │
          [7] The Glass Mere
                │
          [8] The Hall of Doors
                │
          [9] The Welcome Hall (the Seer's Floor, the Luggage, the Steward)
                │
          [10] The Golden Gate
```

Players choose a route from 1 to 5 (two of rooms 2/3/4 is typical). **Steer them toward [4]** if they don't have Emberoak Sap; the Returned can say *"The old ones fought it with fire from the oaks."* 🎲 Roll on **Cave Weirdness** whenever they enter a new room or take a short rest. Roll on **Wandering Monsters** on a d6 roll of 1 when they dawdle.

**Environmental throughline:** Frost on the walls at the top, then wet rock, then warm stone, then *sand* underfoot by room 7. Somewhere below, faint music. Describe it every room. It should feel like walking from January into July.

---

## [1] The Landing
A mound of broken cobblestones, chunks of harbor ice, smoldering bonfire logs and a bewildered festival goat. Three tunnels lead off.
- 🎲 Survival or Investigation: the Gilded Hand's footprints head toward the Fishfolk Camp.
- If the baby mimic is with the party, it gets excited and tugs toward the warmth.

## [2] Bullywug Hollow
A humid cavern of lily pads and croaking. A tribe of *Bullywugs* lives around a dark pond, ruled by the bloated **King Bulgrot the Magnificent**, who lounges on a throne of moldering cushions.
- **The Leaping Pad:** to cross the pond, climb a rock platform and leap onto a giant springy lily pad, then bound onward. Acrobatics DC 12; on a failure you land in the pond and a *Giant Frog* grabs you.
- 🔀 **The King's court:** the bullywugs are vain and easily flattered. Persuasion or Performance earns passage *and* a gift. They're roasting something on skewers. (Don't ask what.)
- They own a **flat two-hulled barge**, salvaged from the Mudlark sewers, and will trade it for something shiny. It's useful at the Glass Mere.

## [3] The Fishfolk Camp
Through the Mudlark sewers lies a busy underground fishing camp of **fishfolk** (*Kuo-toa*), netting pale fish from a warm underground stream. This is where Millhaven's Fisherman's Luck stall buys its catch.
- The fishfolk are friendly traders and will cook you a meal. 🎲 It's delicious. DC 12 CON or poisoned for 1 hour. (They never get sick. They've adapted.)
- 💥 **Clue:** the stream flows *up* from below. It's warm and smells faintly of roasting meat. They call their catch "the Keeper's fish" and leave offerings for "the Old Man of the Lake."
- **Gorm the Garbage Oracle:** in a side chamber lives an *Otyugh*. It's telepathic and *chatty*, and it trades information for interesting garbage. Fighting it is optional and dangerous (CR 5; make that clear, or halve its HP).
- If the Gilded Hand came this way: their camp, a dead scout, and the Mayor's letter 💥 (if not already found).

## [4] The Three Oaks
Three enormous **petrified oaks** grow from the cavern floor, their roots wrapped around an ancient stone **steam hall** built by a forgotten people. Steam pours out. It's *too hot.*
- **Puzzle: The Steam Hall** ([07_Puzzles.md](07_Puzzles.md)). The heat keeps rising every round they stay, but the treasure is inside.
- **The carvings:** on the oaks, figures pour red liquid into a vast mouth, and the mouth recoils. Encourage the party to make a rubbing or a sketch. Arcana or History DC 15 (or Ilsabet Quill later) reads the inscription: *"Fire of the oak, poured within. The Hungry Vale cannot swallow it."*
- Reward: **Emberoak Sap** (3 doses) and the **Oakheart Mace**: a *+1 mace* that can cast *bless* once per day.

## [5] The Toll Bridge
A rickety rope bridge over a deep chasm. Under it lives **Old Mossback**, a troll who collects tolls.
- **Puzzle: The Toll** ([07_Puzzles.md](07_Puzzles.md)). Pay in jokes, secrets, songs, or gold.
- If the Gilded Hand teamed up with the party, Captain Saye betrays them here: he cuts the rope after crossing (🎲 DEX saves).
- 🔀 **The Kobold Sappers:** across the chasm, a band of kobolds has dug in on the far ledge, hoarding salvage from lost adventurers. They start lobbing clay fire-pots the moment anyone sets foot on the bridge. Run it as a **ranged duel** across the gap ([06_Monsters.md](06_Monsters.md)). The troll considers this "not his department."

## [6] The Mother's Grave
A vast, silent cavern. Embedded in the walls is the **fossil of a creature the size of a cathedral**: rows of wood-grained teeth, a stone "lid," a hinge. A **colossal mimic.** Before it stands a crude grave marker, scratched with great care by something that didn't have hands:

> **HERE LIES MOTHER**

- Fresh flowers lie at the grave. Wildflowers, which don't grow underground. Something comes down here to visit.
- 🎲 Nature or Arcana DC 13: *"A mimic. A mother. The scratches are recent. Whatever made this grave is alive, and it's her child."*
- **The Mourners:** the faint ghosts of long-dead guests drift around the grave, humming a slow lullaby. They're harmless unless disturbed. If the party pays respects (a prayer, a song, a moment of silence), one of them whispers: *"The child only wants company. Don't let it keep you."* If attacked: *Specter* ×2.
- **The Gibbering Mouther of Old Voices** lurks in a side alcove, babbling in the voices of guests it has swallowed (see [06_Monsters.md](06_Monsters.md)).

## [7] The Glass Mere
A wide underground lake, perfectly still, ringed with pale sand. The reflections don't quite match. On the far shore: the glow of a door.
- **Puzzle: The Glass Mere** ([07_Puzzles.md](07_Puzzles.md)). The reflections show memories.
- Ways across: the bullywug barge (easiest), swimming (Athletics DC 13, and the reflections grab at you), or the memory path (the puzzle).
- 💥 **Dusty's moment:** his reflection is wearing a white robe with a gold spiral on it, and holding a lodge key. Only he sees it. (Pass him a note.)

## [8] The Hall of Doors
**Puzzle: The Hall of Five Doors** ([07_Puzzles.md](07_Puzzles.md)). Five doors, five sigils, one way through.

⏱️ *If you're short on time, run either the Hall of Doors or the Seer's Floor, not both.*

## [9] The Welcome Hall (Mini-boss)
A pine-paneled hall, impossibly cozy for this deep underground. It smells of woodsmoke and summer. A fire crackles. A brass bell sits on a polished counter beside an open guest ledger.
- **The Seer's Floor** ([07_Puzzles.md](07_Puzzles.md)): the floor is a grid of rune-carved tiles. Only the Seer (Erik) can see which are safe.
- **The Luggage:** a heap of fine leather trunks by the door. *"Please, leave your burdens here."* One of them is a *Mimic*. Whoever touches it is stuck fast (grappled, escape DC 13).
- **The Steward:** a polite attendant with a straw hat, a pressed apron, and the same smile as every other worker in the vale. A reskinned *Doppelganger* that shapeshifts into people the party knows.
- 🔀 Present the invitations calmly and the Steward signs them into the ledger with no fight, handing each guest a **lodge key** carved with a sigil (see Session 3). Refuse or attack and it's a fight.
- **The ledger:** hundreds of names going back centuries. One recent entry is in Dusty's character's handwriting.
- Anyone without an invitation (the 5th player? the Gilded Hand?) gets a smile: *"Not on the list. But there's always room for one more… at the feast."*

## [10] The Golden Gate
Enormous golden doors set into living rock, carved with a perfect spiral. Above them runs an old inscription in the Undercroft script (Ilsabet's notes, or Arcana DC 12: *"Rest here, and be content."*). Warm air seeps through. Music. Laughter. The smell of meat over a fire.
- 💥 Scratched into the stone beside the gate, tiny, by someone desperate, in plain Common: ***"People forget. But they shouldn't."*** Have a player read it aloud, then move on without comment.

> The invitations in your packs begin to hum. The Gate swings open on its own, and sunlight, real and impossible sunlight, pours into the cave. Beyond lies a green hill sloping down to a sparkling lake. Timber lodges sit in a row along the ridge. Out on the water a wooden raft bobs, and something long and yellow curls from it down into the lake. A little golden cart trundles toward you along a gravel path, with no horse pulling it. The old man driving it lifts his hat. "Well, look who made it," he says. "Come in, come in. You must be tired."

**End session.** No level-up. Hand out the **Gate Gifts** over dinner (see [09_Table_Guide.md](09_Table_Guide.md)).

⏱️ **If short on time:** skip [6] and go straight from the Toll Bridge to the Glass Mere. If running *long*, make [9] a non-combat check-in. Only skip [4] if they already have Emberoak Sap.
