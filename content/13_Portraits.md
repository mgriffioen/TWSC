# Portraits

A picture for every NPC and monster, to hold up or put on the table when the party meets them.

- **Official art** is linked from D&D Beyond at full size (1000 px, the largest it serves) wherever the creature looks like the standard monster. The art belongs to Wizards of the Coast; it's linked, not copied. It needs an internet connection.
- **🎨 Prompts** are for everyone the books don't cover, or whose look is specific to this adventure. Each prompt stands on its own: paste it into an image generator as-is. They share one closing style line so the set matches. Save the result as `portraits/<file>.jpg` in the dm-screen repo and add an image line above the prompt (`![Name](portraits/<file>.jpg)`), and it shows here and on the character's stat block.
- The **stat-block art** at the bottom is what the **Stats** sheet shows for a plain monster. A named NPC's sheet shows their own portrait once it exists.
