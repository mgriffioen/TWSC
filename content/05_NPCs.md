# NPCs

Every NPC has a **voice/mannerism hook** (something easy to perform), a **want**, a **secret**, **stats** if they might end up in a fight, and **loot**: what the party finds if they search, rob or defeat them. Stats are built on standard stat blocks (free in the SRD on D&D Beyond) with small changes for this adventure. On the DM screen app, tap **Stats** next to any name below to see the full block, the loot, and a button to add them to initiative.

> **DM trick:** Keep an index card per major NPC with name, voice and one catchphrase. When you introduce someone, put the card face-up on the table. New players remember NPCs far better when they can see the name.

## Major NPCs

### Oldmin Paulus, the Keeper (The Host) — *True villain*
- **Look:** Weathered, sun-browned old groundskeeper in a broad straw hat. Rides a little golden cart that rolls along by itself. No shadow. The cart leaves no tracks.
- **Voice:** Folksy, slow, generous. Laughs at his own jokes. Always asks if you've eaten.
- **Catchphrases:** *"Well, look who made it."* / *"Stay as long as you like. Longer, even."* / *"You can leave any time you like."* (He means it. Nobody ever does.)
- **Want:** Guests who never leave. He's hungry, and (Twist #8) lonely since Mother died.
- **Secret:** He's a puppet on a pseudopod cord. Cut the cord and he goes limp, still smiling.
- **Stats:** Harmless and charming until Phase 2 of the boss fight. Then *Doppelganger* with **40 HP**, plus:
  - **Golden Cart Ram.** +4 to hit, 9 (2d6+2) bludgeoning, and the target is pushed 10 ft.
  - **"Stay a While."** One creature that can hear him makes a DC 14 WIS save or gains +1 Contentment.
- **Loot:** The **golden cart's gilding**: peel it off for 2d10 × 10 gp of real gold leaf (the cart underneath is ordinary wood). His **ring of lodge keys** opens every lodge except the Hound's Hearth.

### Mayor Corwin Vell — *Quest-giver & hidden villain*
- **Look:** Fur-trimmed chain of office, too many rings, one of them a **spiral** signet.
- **Voice:** Politician; laughs at his own jokes; pats shoulders; says "friend" too often.
- **Want:** Keep Millhaven lucky; keep the bargain alive.
- **Secret:** Sends the invitations. Genuinely torn about it, or not at all (your call: tragic or slimy?).
- **Stats:** *Noble* (AC 15, 9 HP), plus:
  - **The Bargain.** In the vale the Keeper protects him: the Heart's pseudopods never target Vell unless he's the last one left.
  - A coward in a fight. He calls for the watch and runs.
- **Loot:** The **spiral signet ring**: proof of the bargain, and the key that opens the Lantern Council vault. The **Council ledger** listing every invitation sent for thirty years (enough to bring down the Council). A purse of 35 gp.

### Tobias Vell — *The one to rescue*
- **Relation:** The Mayor's nephew, nineteen, restless, bored of Millhaven.
- **In Shangri-La:** Blissed out in a hot spring with a drink; speaks only in compliments.
- **Secret:** He wasn't sent. He *volunteered*, to spite his uncle. (Or his uncle sent him *as payment*.)
- **Stats:** *Commoner* (4 HP). In the vale he's at Contentment 5 and fights to stay, unarmed.
- **Loot:** His late mother's **engraved silver pocket watch** (25 gp, but worth far more as a reminder of home: showing it to him gives advantage on the Persuasion check to bring him back). His own **golden invitation**.

### Captain Roderic Saye of the Gilded Hand — *Rival*
- **Look:** Gold-trimmed cloak, a crew in matching colors, a personal banner.
- **Voice:** Smug, sporting, magnanimous in victory, unbearable in defeat. Calls the party "the lower deck."
- **Want:** Glory and treasure, and (Twist #5) to get *back into* Shangri-La.
- **Arc options:** Betrays the party at the Toll Bridge → gets swallowed in the vale → the party can save him in the finale (redemption!) or not.
- **Stats:** *Bandit Captain* (AC 15, 65 HP), plus:
  - **Glory Hound.** When an enemy he can see drops to 0 HP, Saye shouts a boast and has advantage on his next attack roll.
  - **Former Guest (Twist #5).** Advantage on saves against the Keeper's charms.
- **Loot:** The **stolen half of the Undercroft map**. A **+1 scimitar** with a gilded hilt. 2 *Potions of Healing*. A purse of 40 gp. A faded golden invitation from twelve years ago, with his name on it.

#### Wren — *Gilded Hand scout*
- Quick knives. **Stats:** *Spy* (AC 12, 27 HP, Sneak Attack). She flees if Saye falls.
- **Loot:** Two balanced daggers, **thieves' tools**, and a Gilded Hand **signal whistle** (two short blasts call the crew).

#### Bastian — *Gilded Hand sellsword*
- Slow-witted, loyal. **Stats:** *Thug* (AC 11, 32 HP). He fights until Saye says stop.
- **Loot:** A heavy crossbow with 12 bolts, 8 gp, and an unsent letter to his mother promising he'll "be home rich by spring."

#### "Little" Holm — *Gilded Hand muscle*
- A huge, gentle half-ogre who hates the Captain. **Stats:** *Thug* with **52 HP** and STR 19, plus:
  - **Maul.** Two attacks: +6 to hit, 11 (2d6+4) bludgeoning each.
  - **Gentle Giant.** If Saye is down, or a PC is kind to him (Persuasion DC 12), Holm surrenders or switches sides.
- **Loot:** His maul, and a small **carved wooden bird** he's been whittling for someone he won't name. If he joins the party, he gives it to whoever was kindest to him.

### Auntie Grell — *Mudlark boss, the Frost Docks*
- **Look:** Tiny, ancient halfling, on a throne of river junk in a houseboat frozen into the harbor.
- **Voice:** Raspy, calls everyone "duckling." Bargains over a hand of cards and cheats shamelessly.
- **Want:** Leverage. Everything costs a favor.
- **Secret:** Her sister Mira went to Shangri-La sixty years ago. She'll help for free if they promise to look for her. 💥 Mira is in the vale, still twenty years old.
- **Stats:** *Spy* with **22 HP**, plus:
  - **Mudlark Whistle.** Once per day, as a bonus action, she calls a *Swarm of Rats* that arrives at the start of her next turn.
  - **Card Sharp.** Advantage on Sleight of Hand checks to cheat at cards.
- **Loot:** The **iron key to the sewer grate**. A **marked deck of cards** (advantage on checks to cheat with it). A strongbox with 60 gp. Robbing her makes every Mudlark in Millhaven an enemy.

### Brother Ansel — *Leader of the Brotherhood of the Returned, Ham Point*
- **Look:** Hollow-eyed, strangely sunburned in January, smells of smoke.
- **Voice:** Whispers, then suddenly shouts. Flinches at laughter.
- **Want:** For someone to finally end it.
- **Gives:** His last vial of Emberoak Sap.
- **Secret:** Recognizes Dusty's character. *"You… you were in the lodge with the verse over the door. Two down from mine."*
- **Stats:** *Acolyte* (9 HP; *bless*, *cure wounds*, *sanctuary*), plus:
  - **Returned.** Immune to being charmed by the Keeper.
- **Loot:** His **last vial of Emberoak Sap** (he gives it freely). A **book of names** listing everyone the Returned know went missing. Dusty's character is in it.

### Ilsabet Quill — *Cartographer, by the Old Gate*
- **Voice:** "Well, *actually*…"
- **Want:** The other half of the map, and credit on any map of Shangri-La.
- **Useful:** Reads the old Undercroft script.
- **Stats:** *Commoner* (4 HP) with History +4 and Investigation +4. Not a fighter; she'd rather correct your grammar.
- **Loot:** Her **half of the Undercroft map**, **cartographer's tools**, and a brass **reading lens** (25 gp) that gives advantage on checks to read faded writing.

### Old Mossback — *The Toll Bridge troll*
- **Voice:** Deep, slow, scrupulously fair bureaucrat. Has a rubber stamp and a ledger.
- **Want:** Tolls. Rules are rules.
- **Stats:** *Troll* (AC 15, 84 HP, regenerates). Too strong for this party: he only fights people who refuse the toll, and he'd rather shake the bridge.
- **Loot:** His **toll box**: 3d10 × 5 gp in old coins. His **toll ledger**, full of a century of travelers' secrets (including any the party paid him). His **rubber stamp**, which he will miss terribly.

### King Bulgrot the Magnificent — *Bullywug Hollow*
- **Look:** Bloated bullywug in a crown of bent cutlery, lounging on moldering cushions.
- **Voice:** Croaky royal "we." Deeply vain.
- **Want:** Shiny things, flattery, and a straw hat like the ones "the smiling men" wear.
- **Stats:** *Bullywug* with **33 HP**, plus:
  - **Royal Croak** (reaction). When an enemy hits him, up to two bullywugs within 30 ft. move up to half their speed toward that enemy.
- **Loot:** The **crown of bent cutlery** (real silver, 30 gp). Hidden in his cushion throne: a *Potion of Water Breathing*.

### Gorm the Garbage Oracle — *Fishfolk Camp*
- An *Otyugh* with a telepathic voice like a kindly grandmother. Knows everything that has ever floated past it. Payment: interesting garbage.
- **Stats:** *Otyugh* (AC 14, 114 HP). Fighting Gorm is optional and dangerous; halve its HP (57) if the party gets into it.
- **Loot:** Buried in Gorm's midden (DC 13 Investigation, and a DC 12 CON save against the smell): a waterlogged but readable *Spell Scroll of Misty Step*, 2d10 gp, and a single golden invitation, half-digested.

### Snik — *Leader of the kobold sappers*
- **Look:** A kobold in a dented helmet three sizes too big.
- **Voice:** Shrill, bossy, terrified of what's below.
- **Stats:** *Kobold* with **12 HP**, plus:
  - **Fire-Pot.** A clay pot bursts at a point within 120 ft. Each creature within 5 ft. makes a DC 12 DEX save: 7 (2d6) fire, half on a success.
  - **Snik's Big Pot** (1/day). The same, but in a 15-ft radius for 14 (4d6) fire. The kobolds cheer for a full round first.
- **Loot:** **Snik's Big Pot**, if he didn't throw it (one use, as above). His **salvage sack**: 2d10 gp, a +1 shield taken from a lost adventurer, and 50 ft. of silk rope.

### The Steward — *Welcome Hall mini-boss*
- Straw hat, pressed apron, white gloves, the same smile as every vale worker. When threatened, shapeshifts into people the party has met.
- **Stats:** *Doppelganger* (AC 14, 52 HP).
- **Loot:** The **guest ledger** (Dusty's character's handwriting is in it). A brass **service bell** that, when rung in the vale, makes any groundskeeper within earshot stop and wait for instructions for 1 round.

### Mira Grell — *Auntie's sister, a guest for sixty years*
- Twenty years old, sixty years after she arrived. At Contentment 5.
- **Stats:** *Commoner* (4 HP).
- **Loot:** A small **music box** that plays the lullaby the Grell sisters' mother sang. Playing it for Mira gives advantage on the check to bring her home, and Auntie Grell will pay any price for it.

### The Baby Mimic
- Disguised as a frozen fish at the festival. If spared, it follows the party like a stray, shapeshifting into small objects (a mug, a boot, a lantern). The players name it.
- It's the Host's child. It knows the way down and is terrified near the Hound's Hearth.
- **Stats:** *Mimic* at half HP (29).
- **Loot:** If spared, it coughs up one gold coin every morning (it's been eating festival purses). If killed, see the Mimic row in the Monsters loot table.
