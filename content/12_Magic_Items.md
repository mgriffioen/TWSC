# Magic Items — Reference

*Reference only. PCs don't start with a magic item; their item comes from the **Gate Gifts** at Saturday dinner (see [09_Table_Guide.md](09_Table_Guide.md)). Use this page for swaps, Undercroft loot, or a reward when a player earns one.*

All are official **uncommon** items from the *Dungeon Master's Guide* (none would unbalance a level 3 party), and each one has a job somewhere in Millhaven, the Undercroft or the vale.

**Two left off on purpose:** *Bag of Holding* and *Cloak of Protection* already appear elsewhere (the Heart's hoard and Morning Boon 4).

🔗 = requires attunement

## For fighting

| # | Item | What it does | Why it fits this adventure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wand of Magic Missiles** | 7 charges; *magic missile* that never misses. Regains 1d6+1 at dawn. | Auto-hit damage for anyone. Shreds Crawling Claws (the Tidy Hands) before they grab the sap. |
| 2 | **Gauntlets of Ogre Power** 🔗 | Strength becomes 19. | Wrestling free of a mimic's adhesive or a Towel Golem's grapple. |
| 3 | **Bracers of Archery** 🔗 | +2 damage with longbows and shortbows. | Built for the ranged duel with the kobold sappers across the chasm. |
| 4 | **Sentinel Shield** | Advantage on initiative and Wisdom (Perception) checks. | The mimics' whole trick is surprise. This is the anti-ambush item. |
| 5 | **Periapt of Wound Closure** 🔗 | Auto-stabilize at 0 HP; double the HP regained from Hit Dice. | Safety net for a new player on the front line. Pairs well with the Towel Golem towel. |
| 6 | **Pearl of Power** 🔗 | Once a day, regain one spent spell slot (up to 3rd level). | The cleric's or warlock's extra slot for the boss fight. |

## If you hand one out

- **Where they fit:** a swap if a player wants something off the Gate Gift list, a find in the Undercroft, or a reward from an NPC (the Mudlarks' favor, the Returned's thanks).
- **New players:** steer them to items with one obvious use: *Wand of Magic Missiles*, *Sentinel Shield*, *Boots*, *Immovable Rod*, *Gauntlets*.
- **Dusty:** *Ring of Mind Shielding* or *Eyes of Minute Seeing* will surface his secret in interesting ways.
- **Erik (the Seer):** *Medallion of Thoughts* or *Eyes of Minute Seeing* give him more secrets to text about.
- **No duplicates.** First pick wins; let them roll d20 for order if two want the same one.
