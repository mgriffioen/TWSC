# Map Prompts — Image Generator

*DM prep. Paste-ready prompts for the three locations, in two kinds:*
- **📜 Handout maps**: in-world props the players hold. Aged, illustrated, made by someone in the story.
- **⚔️ Battle maps**: top-down tactical maps for minis or Owlbear Rodeo (so Erik can see them too).

## How to use these

1. **Don't let the generator write the labels.** Image models garble text. Every handout prompt asks for *blank* banners and cartouches; the **Labels** list under each one is what to add afterwards in Canva, Photoshop or by hand with a brown pen (the pen looks best).
2. **Don't let it draw the grid either.** It will be crooked. Generate without one, then add a 1-inch grid in Owlbear Rodeo (Erik's view) or overlay a grid before printing. The **Scale** line tells you how many squares the image should span.
3. **Use the style anchor.** Paste the matching anchor at the start of each prompt in the same family so all the maps look like one campaign. If your tool supports a reference image, feed it the first map you like.
4. **Aspect ratios** are given in brackets. Midjourney users: add `--ar 3:2` etc. and `--no text, letters, words, grid, people` to the end. Other tools: put the "Avoid" line in the negative prompt box.
5. **Keep the secrets out.** The handouts deliberately hide the twists. Where a prompt includes a hidden clue, it's marked 🤫 and is meant to be noticed only in hindsight.

### Style anchors

**Handout anchor:**
> Hand-drawn fantasy cartography on aged parchment, brown and sepia iron-gall ink with muted watercolor washes, fine pen hatching, decorative compass rose, ornate empty cartouche banners for labels, small illustrated vignettes of buildings and landmarks, worn creased edges, coffee stains, high detail, print-quality, no legible text.

**Battle map anchor:**
> Top-down orthographic tabletop RPG battle map, viewed from directly overhead, painterly digital art in the style of a premium VTT map, crisp readable terrain, clear walls and floor edges, consistent scale of 5-foot squares, even soft lighting with gentle shadows, no grid, no text, no characters, no tokens, high resolution.

**Avoid (negative prompt, all maps):**
> text, letters, words, labels, watermark, signature, grid lines, people, figures, creatures, perspective view, isometric, tilted camera, blurry, cropped edges

## ❄️ Millhaven (Session 1)

### 📜 Handout — "The Winter Festival Map of Millhaven" `[3:2]`
*A festival broadsheet map the Lantern Council hands out to visitors. Give it out at The Two Decks during Scene 1, or as a prize from the Banner Run.*

> [Handout anchor] A bird's-eye illustrated map of a cold northern harbor city in the dead of winter, snow on every rooftop, the whole harbor frozen solid and white. The city curves around a horseshoe harbor. At the center, a large cobbled market square ringed with bonfires and festival bunting, with a painted archery target and a bell tower. Facing the square, an inn built from an old ship's hull hauled up onto land, with two decks and lit windows. Beside the square, a grand stone council hall with lanterns along its roof. A row of food stalls with little striped awnings. To the north, a rocky headland jutting into the frozen sea, crowded with stone smokehouses and curling smoke, with a steep winding road running down the hill from it (a sled run). Out on the frozen harbor, a ramshackle houseboat locked in the ice beside snowy wooden piers, and a round iron sewer grate in the dock. On the inland edge of the city, an old stone city gate with a narrow tower stacked with map scrolls beside it. Fishing boats frozen in place. A great iron hammer on a platform at the harbor's edge. Tiny decorative skaters, sleds and lantern strings drawn as border ornament. Eight to ten empty banners and cartouches placed next to the landmarks, a title cartouche at the top, and a decorative border of snowflakes and fish. 🤫 The city's winding streets, seen from above, very subtly coil inward toward the square in a spiral.

**Labels to add:** *Millhaven* (title) · *Winter Festival* · Bell Square · The Two Decks · Council Hall · Fisherman's Luck · Ham Point · The Sled Run · The Frost Docks · The Old Gate · Cartographers' Guild · The Great Hammer

## 🕯️ The Undercroft (Session 2)

### 📜 Handout — The Torn Undercroft Map `[2:3]`
*Ilsabet Quill's old map. Print it, then* ***tear it down the middle***: *she gives one half, the Gilded Hand have the other. The map shows the route only as far as the Golden Gate and doesn't say what lives where.*

> [Handout anchor] An ancient underground cave map drawn on cracked, water-stained vellum, inked in faded brown with tiny precise pen notes. It shows a network of caverns connected by winding tunnels, laid out like a flowchart from top to bottom. At the top, a round collapsed shaft with rubble and a narrow stone stair joining it. From there, three tunnels branch to three caverns side by side: on the left a cavern with a round pond and lily pads; in the middle a cavern with a winding stream and small fishing nets; on the right a cavern with three huge trees drawn around a small square stone building. The left and middle tunnels join and lead down to a deep chasm crossed by a thin rope bridge; the right-hand tunnel also joins at the bridge. Below the bridge, a vast cavern containing a huge ribbed shape like an enormous fossil skeleton. Below that, a wide oval lake ringed with sand and dotted with a faint path of stepping stones. Below that, a circular chamber with five doorways around its edge. Below that, a rectangular hall drawn with a floor of small square tiles. At the very bottom, a pair of great doors carved with a spiral, with rays of light drawn coming through them. The margins are crowded with blank note lines, small sketches of runes, and a measured scale bar. Colors fade from frosty blue-grey wash at the top of the map to warm ochre and gold at the bottom. A strong vertical fold crease down the middle of the sheet.

**Labels to add:** *The Undercroft* · The Landing · Ham Point Stair · Bullywug Hollow · Fishfolk Camp · The Three Oaks · The Toll Bridge · *(leave the fossil cavern unlabeled, or write "Here be bones")* · The Glass Mere · The Hall of Doors · The Welcome Hall · The Golden Gate

## ☀️ Shangri-La (Session 3)

### 📜 Handout — "Welcome to Shangri-La" Guest Map `[3:2]`
*The Keeper gives this to each guest with their lodge key. It should look like the cheeriest summer retreat brochure ever made.*

> Vintage hand-painted summer resort map in the style of an old 1950s lake-camp brochure illustration, bright cheerful gouache colors, sunny and idyllic, decorative border of wildflowers and fireflies, ornate empty ribbon banners for labels, no legible text. A green grassy hill slopes down to a small sparkling blue lake. Along the top of the ridge stand seven timber lodges in a neat row, each with a round carved emblem over its door: an open book, a spiral, an open eye, a wave under a roof, a heart, a sun, and a hound. The seventh lodge, with the hound, is set slightly apart and drawn with its shutters closed. A winding gravel path runs along the hill with a tiny golden horseless cart on it. At the bottom of the hill, a cookfire with a long feast table. A smooth bowling green with wooden balls. A little driftwood beach bar with a thatched roof on the shore. A steaming hot spring with a wooden deck. A boathouse on the shore. Out in the middle of the lake, a square wooden raft with a long curving yellow slide chute twisting down into the water. A big golden gate set into a cliff at the edge of the vale, open, with sunlight streaming. Sky-rockets bursting over the lake. A sun low in a perfect golden sky. 🤫 Seen as a whole, the outline of the vale is very subtly shaped like a wide open mouth: the lake sits where the throat would be, the row of lodges curves like a line of teeth, and the yellow chute is shaped like a tongue.
