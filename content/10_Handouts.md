# Items

Player-facing cards for everything the party can find, win or be given. On the DM screen, every entry has a **🖨 Print** button that sends it to SpellMaster as a card you can tear off and hand across the table. Each stat block's Loot section links to the cards it drops.

**How each entry prints:**
- **Type** becomes the card's banner and subtitle.
- **Value**, **Weight** and **Charges** print as rows.
- The quoted text is what the players read.
- **Found** and **Also called** never print. They tell you where it comes from and help the app match it to loot.
- **DM** lines go into SpellMaster's note, which only prints if you tick it.

## More Magic Items (not placed)

Eighteen more official **uncommon** items from the *Dungeon Master's Guide* that fit the adventure. None is placed in the story, and none would unbalance a level 3 party. PCs don't start with a magic item: theirs comes from the **Gate Gifts** at dinner before Session 3 (see the [Table Guide](09_Table_Guide.md)).

**If you hand one out:**
- **Where they fit:** a swap if a player wants something off the Gate Gift list, a find in the Undercroft, or a reward from an NPC (the Mudlarks' favor, the Returned's thanks).
- **New players:** steer them to items with one obvious use, like *Sentinel Shield* or *Immovable Rod* (or the Gate Gift *Wand*, *Boots* and *Gauntlets* above).
- **Dusty:** *Ring of Mind Shielding* or *Eyes of Minute Seeing* will surface his secret in interesting ways.
- **Erik (the Seer):** *Medallion of Thoughts* or *Eyes of Minute Seeing* give him more secrets to text about.
- **No duplicates.** First pick wins; let them roll d20 for order if two want the same one.
