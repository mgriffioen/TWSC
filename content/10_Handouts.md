# Handouts & Loot Cards

Player-facing cards for everything the party can find, win or be given. On the DM screen, every entry has a **🖨 Print** button that sends it to SpellMaster as a card you can tear off and hand across the table. Each stat block's Loot section links to the cards it drops.

**How each entry prints:**
- **Type** becomes the card's banner and subtitle.
- **Value**, **Weight** and **Charges** print as rows.
- The quoted text is what the players read.
- **Found** and **Also called** never print. They tell you where it comes from and help the app match it to loot.
- **DM** lines go into SpellMaster's note, which only prints if you tick it.

## Potions & Consumables

### Potion of Healing
- **Type:** Potion, common
- **Value:** 50 gp
- **Weight:** 1/2 lb
- **Found:** The Luggage, the Gibbering Mouther, Captain Saye, the Morning Boons
- **Also called:** potions of healing
> A small flask of red liquid that glimmers when shaken. When you drink it (an action), you regain **2d4 + 2** hit points.

### Potion of Water Breathing
- **Type:** Potion, uncommon
- **Value:** 100 gp
- **Weight:** 1/2 lb
- **Found:** Hidden in King Bulgrot's cushion throne
> A cloudy green flask that smells of the sea, with a tiny fish floating inside. When you drink it, you can breathe underwater for **1 hour**.

### Emberoak Sap
- **Type:** Alchemical resin, rare
- **Charges:** 1 dose per vial
- **Found:** Brother Ansel (1 dose), the Three Oaks steam hall (3 doses)
> A stoppered vial of thick red resin that's warm to the touch, even in winter. Tapped from the petrified oaks of a forgotten people. A drop on the tongue burns like a live coal.
>
> **Drink:** take 1d4 fire damage; your head clears of any charm or comfort.
> **Coat a weapon** (bonus action): +1d6 fire damage for 1 minute.
> **Throw** it into something that is trying to swallow you.
- **DM:** Hurled into the Heart's maw: 4d10 fire damage and the Heart is stunned until the end of its next turn. Drinking it also lowers Contentment by 2.

### Fire-Pot
- **Type:** Alchemical weapon, common
- **Value:** 10 gp
- **Weight:** 1 lb
- **Found:** Kobold sappers (1d4)
- **Also called:** fire-pots
> A fist-sized clay pot sealed with wax, sloshing with something oily. Light the wick and throw it (range 20/60 ft.). Each creature within 5 ft. of where it bursts makes a **DC 12 Dexterity** save, taking **2d6 fire** damage, or half on a success.

### Snik's Big Pot
- **Type:** Alchemical weapon, uncommon
- **Weight:** 4 lb
- **Found:** Snik, if he didn't throw it
- **Also called:** big pot
> A clay pot the size of a kobold's head, painted with a grinning face and the word "BIG." Light it and throw it (range 20/60 ft.). Each creature within **15 ft.** makes a **DC 12 Dexterity** save, taking **4d6 fire** damage, or half on a success. One use.

### Mimic Glue
- **Type:** Strange substance, common
- **Weight:** 1 lb
- **Found:** Baby mimic or feast mimics (if killed)
> A grey lump that's faintly warm and smells of old wood. Smear it on a door, a chest lid or a boot sole and it sticks fast: a **DC 13 Strength** check pulls it free. One use.

### Dried Keeper's Fish
- **Type:** Food, common
- **Found:** Fishfolk
- **Also called:** keeper's fish
> A bundle of pale dried fish wrapped in reed string. Counts as a day's rations. It tastes wonderful. It tastes like a summer cookout.
- **DM:** Anyone who eats it makes a DC 12 CON save or is poisoned for 1 hour.
